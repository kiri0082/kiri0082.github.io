Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

email scan is a private, personal tool used only by its developer and the people who personally authorize it. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data

email scan requests read-only access to Google user data, only after the account holder signs in and approves it. That data is used solely to provide the tool's features to the person who authorized it.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to any third party, except as needed to provide the tool's features or as required by law. Access tokens are stored only on the developer's own device.

email scan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke email scan's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further use of your data.
